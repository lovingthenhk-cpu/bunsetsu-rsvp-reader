- BudouX 0.9.0 (JavaScript parser and Japanese model): Copyright 2021 Google LLC, Apache License 2.0. See `assets/budoux/LICENSE` and https://github.com/google/budoux.

Local copies avoid requiring third-party script CDNs and external text servers during normal use.
